There's a particular tone most of the web speaks in. It's the tone of the
pop-up that appears before you've read a sentence, the newsletter modal,
the *"You might also like"* rail, the button that pulses to remind you it
exists. It's the voice of a place that's afraid you'll leave.

I wanted this site to speak differently.

## We don't chase the user

Primary navigation is provided exactly once, in the Navigator at the bottom of
the screen. Pages don't redirect your attention back to those same
destinations with hero CTAs. If you want to see the work, you'll find the
way — and the way is calm and obvious.

This sounds like a small thing. It changes everything about how the pages
feel. Without a CTA demanding the next click, a page is allowed to simply
*be read*.

## Quiet beats loud

Color, motion, and weight are spent like punctuation, not wallpaper. A
single handwritten line in the header. A pastel em on one word in a
paragraph. A card that lifts two pixels on hover. None of it shouts,
because the moment everything shouts, nothing is heard.

> The best version of this site probably exists at 5pm, with a cup of tea,
> when nobody's in a hurry.

## Fast *is* a feeling

Being unhurried in tone doesn't mean being slow in fact. The site ships
almost no JavaScript, optimises every image, and swaps pages without a
reload. Calm and fast aren't opposites — a page that loads instantly is
exactly what lets you slow down and read.
