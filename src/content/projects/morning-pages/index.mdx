Morning Pages is a distraction-free journaling app built around one
ritual: write three pages, by hand or by keyboard, first thing in the
morning. No streaks to break, no analytics, no nudges to "engage."

## Why it exists

Most journaling apps optimise for retention. Morning Pages optimises for
*getting out of the way*. The entire interface is a sheet of warm paper,
a cursor, and a quiet progress mark that fills as you write — and then
the app politely closes the day's entry and leaves you alone.

## Notable bits

- A **PWA** so it installs to the home screen and works fully offline.
- Local-first storage; your words never leave the device.
- A typographic measure tuned for long-form comfort, not density.

It's the calmest thing I've built, and the one I use most.
